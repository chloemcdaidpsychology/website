About

I am a UK-based Clinical Psychologist offering private psychological therapy and assessment for adults. My work is grounded in evidence-based practice and a collaborative, compassionate approach.

Sessions are remote and designed to feel supportive, clear, and at your pace — without pressure or judgement.

Who I work with

People come to therapy for lots of reasons — feeling stuck, overwhelmed, anxious, low, or simply wanting to understand themselves better. We’ll take things gently and focus on what matters most to you.

Remote therapy can be a good fit if you want flexibility, privacy, and support that fits around life.

My approach

Therapy is collaborative. We’ll explore what’s going on, make sense of patterns, and find practical ways forward. Sessions are shaped around your goals, your needs, and what feels most helpful to you.

02 · Therapy Tracking the nervous system
02

Tracking the Nervous System

We’re noticing shifts in your breathing, posture, tone, and pace — subtle signs of stress, safety, overwhelm, or shutdown — and adjusting the session to help your body and mind feel regulated.

03 · Therapy Evidence-based support
03

Evidence-based support

I work integratively, drawing from evidence-based approaches (including CBT-informed work and compassion-focused therapy). Sessions are tailored to you, with an emphasis on safety, clarity, and sustainable change.
